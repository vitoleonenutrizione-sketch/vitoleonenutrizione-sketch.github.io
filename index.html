Flagship project

Italian School Meals Observatory

An independent multi-municipality pilot examining published school-menu provision, governance, monitoring, sustainable procurement and the public availability of outcome data.

7municipalities
170coded lunches
12governance domains
10%stratified QA sample
What I built

A reproducible framework, not a ranking.

I developed a codebook, sampling rules, a governance matrix and a QA workflow to compare heterogeneous public information without turning municipalities into a simplistic league table. A source-version discrepancy identified during QA triggered a complete 45-row re-code for Rome, preserving methodological consistency and provenance.
